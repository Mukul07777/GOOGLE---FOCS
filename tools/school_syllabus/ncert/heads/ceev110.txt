Unit 4 Things Around Us About the unit In this unit, we will explore the many things that we make and use to live comfortably. We will observe these items, learn what they are made of, and discover where they come from. We will delve into the natural resources that provide us with these materials. We will also explore various materials and learn how they are used in different ways. Additionally, we will observe the waste materials around us and learn how to manage them to keep our surroundings clean and healthy. This journey will help us understand and appreciate the resources that we use every day. Reprint 2026-27 Note to the Teacher This unit is about ‘Things Around Us’.
