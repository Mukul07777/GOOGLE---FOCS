In the chapters ‘Democracy’ and ‘Elections’, we learnt that democracy is a system of self-governance that involves institutions, rules, and practices that enable people to participate in decision-making. However, in a well-functioning democracy, it is not enough to participate only in elections to form the government. A robust democracy requires transparent institutions, responsible representatives, and the continuous participation of people in public life. Governance Governance in a democratic system is based on transparency, accountability, participation, and the rule of law. Democratic governance places citizens at the centre of the political process. It is more people-centric and welfare-oriented.
