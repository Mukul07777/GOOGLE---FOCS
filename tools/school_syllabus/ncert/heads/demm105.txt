Sharing and Sharing and Measuring Measuring PARTS AND WHOLES Ikra and her little sister, Samina, decide to make a drawing, but they are left with a single drawing sheet. Ikra wants to share the paper by dividing it in half, but Samina insists on having a bigger part of the paper. Ikra thought for a moment and proposed a solution. Would you like half of the paper to draw, or two quarters of the paper? One half paper seems smaller, but two quarters sounds like a bigger part. I want two quarters!
