National Education Policy (NEP) 2020: A Collaborative Journey
The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 is an excellent example of how public 
policy is developed through consultation, expert advice, and public participation. 
India’s previous National Policy on Education was introduced in 1986. 
Over time, society changed rapidly. These changes created 
a need to rethink India’s education system.
Recognising this need, the Government of India initiated 
the process of developing a new education policy in 2015. 
For drafting this policy, the government set up an expert 
committee in 2017, which was chaired by renowned 
scientist Dr K. Kasturirangan. The committee included 
experts from education, science, technology, social 
sciences, and public administration. A unique feature 
of NEP 2020 was the large-scale public consultation. The 
draft policy was made available for feedback by citizens 
and all stakeholders. During the formulation of the policy, 
2.5 lakh Gram Panchayats, 6600 blocks, 6000 Urban Local 
Bodies, and 676 districts were consulted, and about 2 lakhs 
suggestions were received. The expert committee carefully 
examined the suggestions. Several recommendations 
from citizens and institutions were incorporated before 
the final policy was prepared. After discussions within the 
Government of India, the National Education Policy 2020 
was approved by the Union Cabinet in July 2020.
