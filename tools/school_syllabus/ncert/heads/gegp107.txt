A TALE OF THREE INTERSECTING LINES 7 A triangle is the most basic closed shape. As we know, it consists of: • three corner points, that we call the vertex of the triangle, and • three line segments or the sides of the triangle that join the pairs of vertices. Triangles come in various shapes. Some of them are shown below. B C ∆ ABC A X Z Y ∆ YZX ∆WUV V U W ∆BAU A B U Observe the symbol used to denote a triangle and how the triangles are named using their vertices. While naming a triangle, the vertices can come in any order. The three sides meeting at the corners give rise to three angles that we call the angles of the triangle. For example, in ∆ABC, these angles are ∠CAB, ∠ABC, ∠BCA, which we simply denote as ∠A, ∠B and ∠C, respectively.
