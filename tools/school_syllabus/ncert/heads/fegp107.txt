Recall that when some whole number of things are shared equally among some number of people, fractions tell us how much each share is. Shabnam: Each child’s share is 1 4 roti. Mukta: And which is more 1 2 roti or 1 4 roti? Shabnam: When 2 children share 1 roti equally, each child gets 1 2 roti.
