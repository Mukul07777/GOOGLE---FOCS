A long long time ago, there was a cowherd family in Tarakeshwar. Every day Deba and Deep took the cows out for grazing. They used to return in the late evening together with all the cows. One day, Deba asked, “How do we know that we have not lost any cows?” They did not know how to count. What could Deba and Deep do? Did all the cows come back home? Their friend gave them an idea. “When you go out, make a mark on the wall for each cow that leaves the gate.
