The Big Questions Have you ever received pocket money or money from relatives during festivals or special occasions? Think about how you usually use that money.  Do you spend it immediately or save some of it?  Do you keep your savings in a piggy bank (gullak) or somewhere else?  How do you manage expenses, such as travelling to school, buying stationery, or occasionally purchasing food from the school canteen?  Have you ever planned your spending to save for something you wanted later, like visiting your grandparents during summer break? Our journey into managing money begins in childhood. As we grow into adulthood, we earn income, and spend it in many ways to fulfil our needs and wants. We also save money and make investments to achieve goals, such as buying a house or funding higher education. Individuals and households in today’s world have to make many decisions involving money. Money is, therefore, an important resource that should be utilised efficiently. This is where an understanding of personal finance becomes important. Personal finance refers to the planning and management of an individual’s money to meet their needs and wants.
