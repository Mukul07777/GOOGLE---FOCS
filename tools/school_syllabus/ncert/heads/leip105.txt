5.1 Introduction to Computer Networks We are living in a connected world. Information is being produced, exchanged, and traced across the globe in real time. It's possible as almost everyone and everything in the digital world is interconnected through one way or the other. A group of two or more similar things or people interconnected with each other is called network (Figure 5.1). Some of the examples of network in our everyday life include: • Social network • Mobile network • Network of computers • Airlines, railway, banks, hospitals networks.
