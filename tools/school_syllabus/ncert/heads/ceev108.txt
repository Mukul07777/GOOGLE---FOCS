Chhappan bhog is special food prepared on festivals and auspicious occasions in honour of guests, friends, and family. It contains 56 varieties of food items representing a harmonious blend of six tastes: sweet (meetha), spicy (teekha), astringent (kasaila), sour (khatta), salty (namkeen) and bitter (karva).
