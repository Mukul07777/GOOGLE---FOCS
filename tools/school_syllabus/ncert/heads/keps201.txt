1 Chapter 1: Constitution: Why and How? Chapter One CONSTITUTION: WHY AND HOW? INTRODUCTION This book is about the working of the Indian Constitution. In the chapters that follow, you will read information about various aspects of the working of our Constitution. You will learn about the various institutions of the government in our country and their relationship with each other. But before you begin to read about elections, governments, and presidents and prime ministers, it is necessary to understand that the entire structure of the government and the various principles that bind the institutions of government have their origin in the Constitution of India. After studying this chapter, you will learn: ± what a constitution means; ± what a constitution does to the society; ± how constitutions govern the allocation of power in society; and ± what was the way in which the Constitution of India was made.
