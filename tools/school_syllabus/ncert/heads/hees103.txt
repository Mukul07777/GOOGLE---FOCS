Reprint 2026-27 62 Exploring Society: India and Beyond | Grade 8 Part 1 Who are the Marathas? The Marathas are a group of people native to the Deccan plateau, more specifically present-day Maharashtra. They are identified with the language they speak — Marathi — which has had a rich and continuous literary history since the 12th century. In this chapter, we will see them rise as a powerful political entity that will alter the course of India’s history.
