# Questions from open datasets

`scripts/import_open_banks.py` adds questions from these sets to the question bank. Each is published under the
MIT License, which allows commercial use as long as the copyright and permission notice below is kept. Focs writes
its own solution for every question and checks the answer against the set's key before a student sees it.

| Set | What Focs takes | Link |
|---|---|---|
| neet-biology-qa, by sweatSmile | 793 NEET Biology MCQs with answers (not past exam questions; stored as source `open`) | https://huggingface.co/datasets/sweatSmile/neet-biology-qa |
| jee-neet-benchmark, by Reja1 | NEET 2024, 2025 and 2026 and JEE Advanced 2024 to 2026 papers: question images and official answers | https://huggingface.co/datasets/Reja1/jee-neet-benchmark |
| JEEBench, by Daman Arora et al. | JEE Advanced 2016 to 2023 questions (text) and answers | https://huggingface.co/datasets/daman1209arora/jeebench |

The past exam questions themselves were set by the National Testing Agency (NEET, JEE Main) and the IITs (JEE
Advanced). NEET 2020 to 2023 papers are read from the free PDFs on Aakash's site
(https://www.aakash.ac.in/neet-previous-year-question-papers): only the questions and the answers are taken, never
their solutions.
