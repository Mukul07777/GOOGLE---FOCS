5.1 Is This a Multiple Of? Sum of Consecutive Numbers Anshu is exploring sums of consecutive numbers. He has written the following — Now, he is wondering — • “Can I write every natural number as a sum of consecutive numbers?” • “Which numbers can I write as the sum of consecutive numbers in more than one way?” • “Ohh, I know all odd numbers can be written as a sum of two consecutive numbers. Can we write all even numbers as a sum of consecutive numbers?” • “Can I write 0 as a sum of consecutive numbers? Maybe I should use negative numbers.” Explore these questions and any others that may occur to you. Discuss them with the class. Take any 4 consecutive numbers. For example, 3, 4, 5, and 6.
