STEINMETZv 7.1 Introduction In earlier classes, we have learnt how to find the squares and cubes of binomials like a + b and a – b. Using them, we could evaluate the numerical values of numbers like (98)2 = (100 – 2)2, (999)3 = (1000 – 1)3, etc. However, for higher powers like (98)5, (101)6, etc., the calculations become difficult by using repeated multiplication. This difficulty was overcome by a theorem known as binomial theorem. It gives an easier way to expand (a + b)n, where n is an integer or a rational number.
