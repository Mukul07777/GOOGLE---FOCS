Let us take an example of a school that maintains data about its students, along with their attendance record and guardian details. The class teacher marks daily attendance of the students in the attendance register. The teacher records ‘P’ for present or ‘A’ for absent against each student’s roll number on each working day. If class strength is 50 and total working days in a month are 26, the teacher needs to record 50 × 26 records manually in the register every month. As the volume of data increases, manual data entry becomes tedious.
