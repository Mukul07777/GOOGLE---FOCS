We can sort a collection of numbers in ascending (increasing) or descending (decreasing) order. If the collection is of strings, we can sort it in an alphabetical order (a-z or z-a) or according to the length of the string. For example, words in a dictionary are sorted in alphabetical order; seats in an examination hall are ordered according to candidates’ roll number. We can also sort a list of students based on their height or weight. Imagine finding the meaning of a word from a dictionary that is not ordered. We will have to search for the word on each page till we find the word, which will be very tedious.
