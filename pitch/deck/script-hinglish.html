Part 1: Slides, before the video

Slide 1: Title 20 s

"Hello Sharks! Hum hain [naam], Team Focs se.

Aur hum aaye hain ₹[amount] ke liye, [x]% equity ke badle.

Focs ek line mein: lecture ko student tak le jaata hai, aur student ka confusion wapas teacher tak.

Bas ek minute problem pe, phir aapko product live dikhate hain."

Slide 2: The problem 35 s

"Sharks, socho Kota ki koi bhi coaching class, ya koi bhi online batch.

Ek teacher. Ek board. Do sau students. Aur sawaal? Zero.

Teen problems hain. Pehli, Speed. Board aage nikal jaata hai. Ya toh likho, ya suno; dono saath nahi hota.

Doosri, Language. Lakhon bachche Hindi ya Hinglish mein sochte hain, par notes aur doubt solvers sab English mein.

Aur teesri, sabse badi: Return. Jo bachcha lost hai, woh haath nahi uthata. Teacher ko kabhi pata hi nahi chalta ki kaunse board pe class haath se nikal gayi.

Aur yeh har saal hota hai 14 lakh se zyada JEE, 23 lakh NEET, aur 13 lakh UPSC aspirants ke saath."

Slide 3: The gap 20 s

"Ek taraf teacher: apni speed, apni language, aur use dikhta nahi kaun lost hai.

Doosri taraf student: use chahiye apni speed, apni language, aur woh kabhi bolega nahi ki woh lost hai.

Yeh do sides kabhi milti hi nahi. Focs beech ka bridge hai, chaar lanes ke saath."

Slide 4: Vision 25 s

"Humara vision: India ka har lecture, har student ko, uski apni language mein samajh aaye.

Hum teachers ko replace nahi karte, aur hum ek aur content app bhi nahi hain. Focs chalta hai usi lecture ke upar jo student already dekh raha hai: YouTube, PW, Unacademy, Zoom, ya real classroom, desk pe rakhe phone se.

Lane 1, Speed: board aur teacher ki awaaz, live notes aur captions ban jaate hain. Lane 2, Language: Hindi ya Hinglish, formulas bilkul safe. Lane 3, Understanding: har answer pe 'Sure' ya 'Guessing', taaki blind spots pakde jaayein. Lane 4, Return: confusion wapas teacher tak, live."

Slide 5: How it works 35 s · technical depth; your strongest tech speaker

"Aur Sharks, ek baat clear kar dein: yeh ChatGPT wrapper nahi hai.

Capture: Focs sirf ek browser tab share karta hai, board pe crop karta hai. Device pe hi ek chhota change-detector hai, jo frame tabhi bhejta hai jab board pe kuch naya likha jaaye. Matlab khaali board ka cost: zero rupaye.

Read: yahan humara apna model hai, FocsOCR. Humne khud train kiya, 17.6 lakh board lines pe, ek laptop GPU pe. Test frames pe iska error sirf 0.48% hai, jabki PP-OCRv4 ka 7.27%. PP-OCRv4 ek widely used open-source OCR hai.

Write: ek AI router notes, quizzes aur captions likhta hai, har call metered, aur offline fallback ke saath. Return: live rooms 'I'm lost' ko teacher tak le jaate hain.

Aur notes students ke beech share hote hain, toh ek popular lecture pe pehle student ke liye 8 AI calls, aur agle student ke liye zero.

Bas, bahut baatein ho gayi. Ab dekhiye."

Part 3: Slides, after the video

Slide 7: Execution 25 s

"Sharks, jo aapne abhi dekha, woh real app hai, live server pe chal raha hai. Prototype nahi, Figma nahi. Bas accounts sample hain.

37,000 lines of code. 221 automated tests, har push pe. 193 API endpoints, 45 screens. Web app bhi, aur Android app bhi.

Har AI-written question pehle ek doosra model solve karke check karta hai, tab jaake student tak pahunchta hai."

Slide 8: Business model 35 s

"Paisa kaise banega? Starter free hai, taaki har student aaj raat ke lecture pe try kar sake.

Pro sirf ₹149 mahina, ek coaching test series se bhi kam. Aur sabse bada point: Pro mein har hafte ek live mentor session included hai. Saath mein 10 guna AI, roz do full mocks, answer grading aur doubt solver.

Upar se mentor marketplace: one-on-one call ₹49 se shuru, aur Focs 20% rakhta hai. Aur institutes teacher side ke liye pay karte hain: confusion maps aur batch analytics.

Payments already built-in hain, Razorpay se: UPI, cards, netbanking."

Slide 9: Mentor flywheel 25 s

"Mentors humari supply side hain, aur humara moat bhi. Woh proof ke saath apply karte hain, hum har ek ko verify karte hain, approve karte hain, aur proof delete kar dete hain.

Woh chat karte hain, weekly check-ins dekhte hain, calls aur master sessions lete hain, aur 80% kamaate hain. Aur kyunki bahut se students 18 se kam ke hain, phone numbers hide rehte hain, aur har chat report ho sakti hai."

Slide 10: Why Focs 20 s

"Coaching apps apne lectures bechte hain. ChatGPT class ke baad help karta hai, ek-ek screenshot karke.

Focs akela hai jo kisi bhi lecture pe live chalta hai, apne model se board padhta hai, Hindi mein formulas safe rakhta hai, teacher ko batata hai kaun lost hai, aur student ko unse jodta hai jo exam already clear kar chuke hain."

Slide 11: The ask 30 s

"Toh Sharks, hum maang rahe hain ₹[amount], [x]% equity ke liye.

40% AI aur hosting pe, 25% pehle 100 mentors ko recruit aur verify karne pe, 20% proof pe (real classroom boards pe OCR benchmark, aur students ke saath ek learning study), aur 15% coaching centres ke saath pilots pe.

Agle 90 din mein: live hosting, 100 verified mentors, 1,000 students, aur real-world numbers, lab numbers ke saath, imaandari se publish."

Slide 12: Close 10 s

"Every lecture, understood. Focs: lecture student tak, aur confusion wapas teacher tak.

Thank you, Sharks. Ab aapke sawaal."

Stage tips

  • Say the ask line twice: on slide 1 and on slide 11, with confidence and without hesitating.
  • One person speaks over the video, one owns slide 5 (tech), and one handles the ask and the questions.
  • Rehearse with the video at least twice. The voice should lead the screen slightly, not trail it.
