Here we go… Very little is known about Aśhoka’s successors to the throne. It is generally accepted, however, that the last Maurya emperor was assassinated around 185 BCE by his commander- in-chief Puṣhyamitra Śhunga. This led to a breakup of the empire — hardly half a century after Aśhoka, as we mentioned in the last chapter. Many new kingdoms emerged across the subcontinent, which, often, were earlier tributary kingdoms under the overlordship of the Maurya empire.
