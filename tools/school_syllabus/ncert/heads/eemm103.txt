Note for Teachers: Encourage the learners to play the “statue” game. Learners can be asked to rotate themselves around and stop as the teacher announces “statue”. The learners can notice how much they have turned with respect to their original position. Can you recognise the child in the picture who has made a full turn? Who has made a half turn? How do you know? Discuss in class. Give examples of real-life situations where you observe turns.
