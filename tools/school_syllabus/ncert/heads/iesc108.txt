In these distant yet remarkably parallel civilisations, profound thinkers, such as Acharya Kanada in India, and Leucippus and Democritus in Greece, pondered over the same fundamental question that has continued to inspire human inquiry across centuries — What is everything made up of? Acharya Kanada suggested that if matter (dravya) is divided repeatedly, you will reach a stage where you would encounter the smallest particles that can no longer be divided.
